# NORTHMINE Public Demo Access

## Product

NORTHMINE Intelligence is an operational control and decision system for
open-pit mining. The public experience explains the product, shows its
operational scope, and lets qualified visitors request controlled access to an
interactive demo.

## Audience

- Mine operations leaders
- Dispatch and control room teams
- Production and maintenance planners
- Technology and data teams evaluating operational integration

## Core Promise

Connect shift production, fleet condition, loading units, risk, and plan
variance so a decision can be made with evidence and later traced to its
result.

## Public Demo Boundaries

- Operational values are synthetic and representative.
- The public demo has no connection to operational SQL databases.
- Real integrations are configured only in private environments.
- Access is reviewed manually.
- The landing never publishes shared credentials.

## Primary Journeys

1. Understand NORTHMINE in less than one minute.
2. Review operational capabilities and product evidence.
3. Understand what is and is not real in the public demo.
4. Submit a demo access request.
5. Return to the existing login after approval.

## Success Criteria

- Landing is public while the internal application stays protected.
- Request flow is accessible, responsive, and rate limited.
- Requests are stored separately from operational and user data.
- The existing login, SPA fallback, health route, and internal deep links
  continue to work.
